# Demo script (about 3 minutes)

1. **Login** (10s). Open the live URL, sign in as the HR Manager. Point out the demo-data notice.
2. **Directory** (40s). Scroll the 10,000-row directory. Type "abbott" in search; note the debounced results. Filter to India and sort by salary high to low: local INR amounts with labelled USD conversion. Change the department filter; show the URL updating and the back button restoring the previous view.
3. **Employee record** (40s). Open an employee. Show current salary with "since" date, contact and hire date, and the salary history table with who recorded each change.
4. **Salary change** (30s). Click "Change salary", enter a higher amount with today's date and a note. Show the new row marked Current, the old row preserved, and the converted USD figure. Try a future date to show the validation message.
5. **Edit and immutability** (20s). Click "Edit profile"; change the job title; note that country is disabled with the explanation.
6. **Add employee** (30s). Add a new employee with an initial salary; show the currency hint following the chosen country. Submit and land on the new record.
7. **Dashboard** (40s). Headcount, total payroll, median and average, all labelled as converted USD. Toggle the breakdown between country and department; hover a bar for the tooltip. Show highest and lowest paid lists linking back to records.
8. **Close** (10s). Mention the requirements doc and design notes in the repository.
